No software or data license has yet been selected for this repository.

This is a legal and authorship decision, so the cleanup process deliberately
does not assume MIT, BSD, GPL, CC BY, or another license. Until the author adds
a license, normal copyright applies and public visibility alone does not grant
reuse rights.

Before making an archival release, the author should confirm that she has the
right to license every included component, especially:

- modified BookSim source files;
- copied or derived third-party code;
- gem5-related code backups; and
- data or figures subject to co-author or institutional policies.

It may be appropriate to use separate licenses for code and research data, but
that choice should be made with the supervisor/co-authors or institutional
guidance.
